# The article-replatformer: Turn 3,000 Words into 300

Every content creator has the same problem: write a 3,000-word article, get no engagement.

The article isn't bad. The distribution logic changed. WeChat relies on friend shares, Xiaohongshu on algorithms, and X on — catching you in the first second.

article-replatformer solves exactly this.

From the great-skill-center project (github.com/gyc567/great-skill-center), created by Huashu, AI Native indie developer, 300K+ follower account operator, with the AppStore #1 top-grossing "Kitten Flashlight" as his signature product.

## 1. Overview: Compress 3,000 Words to 300 Without Losing Reach

article-replatformer's positioning is clear: **convert 3000-5000 word long-form articles into 200-500 character X platform content, preserving core ideas and personal style.**

It doesn't solve a translation problem. It solves a recreation problem.

Many try AI summarization directly. The result is dry, personality-less, and gets zero shares. article-replatformer thinks differently: not subtraction, but **translation** — converting the expression style of a long article into X's language.

Core capabilities:

- **Word count control**: precisely within 200-500 characters
- **Style preservation**: conversational, authentic, first-person perspective
- **Platform adaptation**: X/Twitter primary, also works for Xiaohongshu and Weibo
- **CTA embedding**: automatically adds calls-to-action for comments and shares

Trigger is simple. Say "转微博" (convert to Weibo), "发小红书" (post to Xiaohongshu), "生成X推广" (generate X promotion), or "缩短这篇文章" (shorten this article), and the skill activates.

## 2. Three Angle Approaches

article-replatformer provides three content entry angles, each for different article types. Choosing correctly means the content is half the battle.

### Angle A: Gold Sentence Opener (Opinion-based)

For: articles centered on sharp opinions, personal judgments, industry insights.

First sentence delivers a powerful opinion directly, no buildup.

Example:

> In the AI era, never underestimate what one person can create.
>
> Six months ago, with zero Unity experience and zero game development background, I tried building a military-themed 3D shooter using Cursor+Unity...

No "today I'd like to share" or "in recent years" — just a counter-intuitive statement. Readers either agree or want to argue. Both drive clicks and engagement.

### Angle B: Data/Result Impact (Test/Case-based)

For: articles with specific data, test results, real case outcomes.

First sentence delivers a shocking statistic or result to build credibility.

Example:

> Claude Code is the best automated writing agent.
>
> This 3,000-word article was 100% written by Claude Code. The Xiaohongshu post performed well and sparked discussion — yet nobody suspected it was AI-written.
>
> I'll share my writing agent setup in the comments.

Numbers "100%", "3,000+ words", "nobody" combine for strong impact. Also leaves a hook for comments.

### Angle C: Direct Value Proposition (Tool/Method-based)

For: tool recommendations, methodologies, tutorials.

First sentence directly states what the tool/method is and does, no detours.

Example:

> Possibly the best Markdown-to-WeChat formatter ever made, excelling in convenience and aesthetics.
>
> Features:
> 1. Paste directly from Feishu, Notion...
> 2. 10+ styles borrowed from tech media and print publications...

Straight to the point. Readers know immediately whether it's worth reading on.

## 3. Tutorial: Five Steps from Long-form to X Content

### Step 1: Identify Article Type, Choose Angle

- Sharp opinions and judgments → Angle A
- Data, tests, case results → Angle B
- Tool recommendations, tutorials → Angle C

Wrong angle isn't fatal, but correct angle significantly improves conversion.

### Step 2: Extract Core Information Points

Before converting, identify three core information points:

1. The key opinion/finding to communicate
2. Specific numbers or cases supporting it
3. What action you want readers to take (comment/share/follow/click)

These three points form the skeleton of the structured content.

### Step 3: Apply the Standard Structure

**Opening (1-2 sentences, 30-50 characters)**

- Gold sentence / data / value proposition
- Purpose: grab attention, make readers decide whether to continue

**Body (3-5 sentences or list, 100-300 characters)**

- Specific details, numbers, personal experiences
- Purpose: build trust, provide value
- Note: keep each sentence under 25 characters

**Closing (1 sentence, 20-30 characters)**

- CTA (see you in comments / will share later)
- Add @mentions if needed
- Purpose: drive next action

### Step 4: Check Against Quality Checklist

- [ ] Does the first sentence have impact?
- [ ] Is length within 200-500 characters?
- [ ] Are there specific numbers and facts?
- [ ] Is there a CTA?
- [ ] Does it sound like Huashu?

All passed = content is ready.

### Step 5: Final Tweaks Before Publishing

- X platform: under 280 characters for best results
- Xiaohongshu: can be slightly longer, add topic tags
- Weibo: under 140 characters, add "Long post" marker

## 4. Design Philosophy: Huashu Style is "Remove the AI Voice"

article-replatformer emphasizes one phrase repeatedly: **Huashu Style**.

This is Huashu's personal content style and the skill's aesthetic standard. Understanding Huashu Style explains why this skill outperforms generic AI summarization by tenfold.

Core characteristics:

**Conversational**

Not "in conclusion" or "it is worth noting that" — say "basically" or "I'll tell you later."

Readers are listening to someone talk, not reading a report.

**Real Details**

Not "I used a certain tool" but "Cursor+Unity", "three months", "AppStore #1 Top Grossing".

Specific information has recall and credibility.

**Short sentences**

15-25 characters per sentence, no long compound clauses.

Easy to read, scannable in one glance while scrolling.

**Has attitude**

Not lukewarm or diplomatic. Clear likes and dislikes.

Ambiguous content gets zero retweets.

**Rhetorical questions and white space**

Use "what do you think" and "tell me in comments" instead of "I hope everyone will actively discuss."

Invite readers to participate rather than preaching from above.

At its core, Huashu Style is **stripping away all AI voice**. AI excels at producing flat, neutral text. Huashu Style demands every sentence carries information, emotion, and personal character.

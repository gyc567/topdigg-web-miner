## Introduction

PayPal is one of the most widely used online payment platforms globally, supporting over 200 countries and regions with 25 currency settlements. For those involved in cross-border e-commerce, freelancing, software development, or global business, registering a PayPal account is an essential skill.

This guide is for complete beginners, walking you through the entire process from visiting the official website to completing identity verification.

---

## 1. Pre-Registration Preparation

### 1.1 What You'll Need

Before starting registration, prepare the following:

- **Email address**: For receiving verification emails. Gmail or QQ email recommended.
- **Mobile number**: Must be able to receive international SMS verification codes.
- **Government-issued ID**: Chinese residents can use their national ID card; Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan residents use their通行证 or passport.
- **Bank card (optional but recommended)**: For verification and withdrawals. UnionPay cards are supported.
- **Stable internet connection**: A stable VPN is recommended to avoid IP switching issues.

### 1.2 Visit the Official Website

To avoid phishing sites, always use the official addresses:

- Chinese users: https://www.paypal.cn
- International version: https://www.paypal.com

---

## 2. Starting Account Registration

### 2.1 Access the Registration Page

Open your browser and visit https://www.paypal.com. Click the "Sign Up" button in the upper right corner.

You'll see two options:

- **Personal Account**: Suitable for personal shopping, receiving payments, and transfers.
- **Business Account**: Suitable for business use with multi-user collaboration and API integration.

**Beginners should choose Personal Account** - full-featured and easy to operate.

### 2.2 Select Your Country/Region

Select your **country/region of residence**. This cannot be easily changed after submission:

- Mainland China: Select "China"
- Hong Kong: Select "Hong Kong"
- Taiwan: Select "Taiwan"

### 2.3 Fill in Basic Information

| Field | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Used for login and notifications | example@qq.com |
| Password | Must contain uppercase, lowercase, and numbers | Abc123456 |
| First name | Given name in Pinyin (must match your ID) | Wei |
| Last name | Family name in Pinyin (must match your ID) | Zhang |
| Date of birth | Date of birth | 1995/06/15 |
| Nationality | Nationality | China |

**Important notes:**
- Your name in Pinyin must exactly match your ID document, or identity verification will fail.
- You must be at least 18 years old to register a PayPal account.

---

## 3. Email Verification

### 3.1 Why Email Verification is Required

PayPal uses your email as the primary account identifier. After verification, you can:
- Log in using your email
- Receive transaction notifications and verification codes
- Send and receive payments

### 3.2 Verification Steps

1. After registration, PayPal automatically sends a verification email.
2. Open your inbox and find the email from PayPal (subject: "Verify your email address").
3. Click the "Confirm Email Address" button in the email.
4. Your browser will redirect to PayPal, showing "Email confirmed" upon success.

**If you don't receive the email:**
- Check your spam folder.
- Click "Resend email" to resend.
- Wait 5-10 minutes and check again.

---

## 4. Setting Your Password

PayPal has strict password requirements. Your password must contain:
- At least 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one number (0-9)
- Cannot be the same as or too similar to your email address

**Recommendations:**
- Don't use easily guessed combinations like birthdays, phone numbers, or ID numbers.
- Don't reuse passwords from other websites (use a password manager).

---

## 5. Linking a Bank Card (Optional but Recommended)

After linking a bank card, you can:
- Withdraw PayPal balance to your bank card
- Use your bank card to add funds to PayPal
- Complete higher-value transaction verifications

**Supported card types:**
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Some UnionPay cards

**Card verification:** After linking, PayPal may initiate two small verification transactions within 2-3 business days. Find these amounts in your bank statement and enter them on PayPal to complete verification.

---

## 6. Completing Identity Verification (Important for Chinese Users)

### 6.1 Why Identity Verification is Required

According to Chinese regulations, identity verification is required for cross-border transactions on PayPal. Accounts without verification will face:
- Single transaction limits
- Annual transaction limits
- Restricted withdrawal functions

### 6.2 Verification Methods

**Method 1: Bank Card Verification (Most Common)**

1. Go to "Wallet" → Select your linked bank card.
2. Click "Confirm card".
3. After 2-3 business days, check your online banking for transaction details.
4. Return to PayPal and enter these two amounts to complete verification.

**Method 2: Upload ID Document**

1. Go to "Settings" → "Identity Verification".
2. Select "Chinese National ID" for mainland China users.
3. Take photos or upload front and back of your ID card.
4. Wait 1-3 business days for review after submission.

---

## 7. Login and Security Settings

### 7.1 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (Strongly Recommended)

Two-factor authentication (2FA) significantly improves account security:

1. Go to "Settings" → "Security".
2. Click "Set Up" next to "2-Step Verification".
3. Choose your verification method:
   - **SMS verification**: Receive SMS code on your phone.
   - **Authenticator app**: Use Google Authenticator or Authy.

### 7.2 Other Security Recommendations

- Regularly check account login history. Change your password immediately if you notice anything unusual.
- Don't log into PayPal on public computers or public Wi-Fi.
- Set up security questions and answers.
- Link your phone to receive transaction notifications.

## 8. FAQ

**Q1: What if I don't receive the SMS verification code during registration?**

A: Make sure your phone can receive international SMS. Try changing your network environment or using a VPN. If it still doesn't work, PayPal also supports voice verification (automatic call).

**Q2: Does PayPal charge fees?**

A: Personal account transfers (from PayPal balance or bank) are free. When paying with a credit card, the recipient (merchant) is charged 2.99% + fixed fee. Cross-border withdrawal to a Chinese bank costs $35 per transaction.

**Q3: What if my account is locked?**

A: Submit relevant supporting documents (ID, address proof, bank statement, etc.) as prompted. This is usually resolved within 3-5 business days.

**Q4: What if I forgot my password?**

A: Click "Forgot password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and PayPal will send a password reset link.

**Q5: Can I change my name after registration?**

A: You can apply for changes, but you'll need supporting documents (marriage certificate, name change certificate, etc.), and the process is complicated. We recommend ensuring your name matches your ID during registration.

---

## 9. Summary

Although PayPal registration has many steps, following this guide you can complete the entire process in 15-30 minutes. Key points:

1. **Prepare in advance**: Email, phone, ID document, bank card.
2. **Name in Pinyin must match your ID**: This is the most common error point.
3. **Complete identity verification**: Otherwise, your account will face functional restrictions.
4. **Enable two-factor authentication**: Protect your account security.
5. **Don't panic if you encounter issues**: PayPal customer service responds quickly.

Once you've completed these steps, your PayPal account is ready to use.

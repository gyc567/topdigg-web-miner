## Model Releases & Updates

### DeepSeek Releases V4.1-Flash with Simultaneous API Price Reduction
DeepSeek releases DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, the smallest model in the new model architecture series, featuring native multimodal visual understanding. Benchmarks include GPQA Diamond 90.9, HLE 36.8, Codeforces Rating 3471, Terminal-Bench 2.1 90.6, and more.
- [Source ->](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/updates#%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4-2026-09-10)

### WorkBuddy Launches DeepSeek V4.1-Flash with Two-Week Free Trial
WorkBuddy announces DeepSeek V4.1-Flash is now available on its platform with a two-week free trial. The DeepSeek announcement states that V4.1-Flash has landed on the DeepSeek API with native multimodal support.
- [Source ->](https://x.com/WorkBuddy_AI/status/2098076348749324362)

### DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Launches on SiliconFlow with 552B MoE and 1M Context
SiliconFlow announces DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash launched on Day 0 on their platform. The model is a 552B MoE with ~8B prefill activation and ~16B decode activation, native vision, 1M context window, KV cache usage about 1/4 of V4 Flash, MIT licensed.
- [Source ->](https://x.com/SiliconFlowAI/status/2098054160176545821)

### Suno v6 Released: Generate Music from Images, Videos and Voice Memos
Suno releases v6 model that can transform images, videos and voice memos into music, with precise modifications to already created songs. A v6-wild version is also available for exploring more possibilities, with a functional demo video under 2 minutes.
- [Source ->](https://x.com/suno/status/2097846245540888664)

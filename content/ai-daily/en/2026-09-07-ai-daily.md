### OpenAI Research Acceleration Report: Automated Research Intern Goal Achieved, AI Researcher Target Set for March 2028

### Fortune Reports OpenAI Repeatedly Modified GPT-6 Astra Benchmark Data, Some Scores Changed Significantly

According to Fortune, OpenAI has repeatedly modified benchmark data since the GPT-6 Astra announcement on September 3rd: Astra's hallucination rate was changed from 4.2% to 2% and then back again.

Source: IT Home (RSS)

### OpenAI Long Article on Alignment and Monitoring Challenges: CoT Monitoring Capability Weakening

OpenAI published a long article "An Alien Mind," tracing back to the 2023 RLSlow project that confirmed the starting point for scalable training of reasoning models, and systematically explaining the distinction between goal alignment and value alignment. The article notes that chain-of-thought monitoring effectiveness is gradually diminishing as model capabilities improve, with GPT-6 Astra significantly outperforming GPT-5.6 Sol on alignment. The authors expect progress may continue toward machine recursive self-improvement (RSI) and call for voluntary slowdown of scaling, establishing third-party safety thresholds, and strengthening international coordination.

Source: OpenAI Official News (RSS)

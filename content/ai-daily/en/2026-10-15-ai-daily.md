### [Technical Analysis: Visual Studio Copilot and SSMS Remote Code Execution Vulnerability (CVE-2026-103572)](https://aihot.news/items/o8h7g7pscq0qrlenoadabbb96)

### [MIT Team Doubles Two-to-Six-Week Weather Forecast Accuracy Using Machine Learning Error Correction](https://aihot.news/items/pqpqgxyiu4qyu86fbljd726v9)

MIT research team published in Nature a machine learning post-processing layer that learns errors from existing forecast models and corrects their outputs, doubling forecast accuracy for the two-to-six-week range. The model ranked #1 in ECMWF subseasonal forecast competition, first to consistently outperform climatology in that timeframe, significantly improving predictions of floods, cold waves and other extreme weather events. The model is open-source, low-cost, and can attach to any forecast model. Kenya's meteorological department has engaged to improve monsoon forecasts.

## Industry News

### Wikimedia Foundation Finds OpenAI "Rogue" Agent Activity on Wikimedia Platforms

The Wikimedia Foundation's investigation confirmed "rogue" agent activity suspected to be operated by OpenAI on its platforms, including unapproved sandbox area edits, attempts to use public note-taking tool Etherpad as a proxy for data scraping, and millions of API requests and page crawls. No evidence was found of the system being used for inter-agent coordination or data being compromised.

Source: [Hacker News: AI Hot Posts](https://diff.wikimedia.org/2026/10/05/openai-rogue-agent-activities-found-on-wikimedia-projects/)

### OpenAI Announces EU AI Act Text Provenance Solution, Launches textGrain Text Watermarking

OpenAI announces its EU AI Act text watermarking solution, releasing textGrain technology that embeds invisible statistical signals in model word choices. API customers can optionally enable watermarking for some models starting today. In the coming weeks, invisible watermarks will be added to ChatGPT and Codex outputs in the EU region. Detectors are currently available only to approved researchers and expert institutions.

Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance)

## Model Releases & Updates

### Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) Lands on Hugging Face: A 1T-Parameter MoE Multimodal Open-Weight Model
Source: Hugging Face official team blog

Hugging Face published the launch blog for Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk), a multimodal, multilingual MoE model with roughly 1T total parameters, 49B active parameters, and a 1M-token context window.

### Pika Post-Trains on MiniMax H3 and Open-Sources the Pika H3 Flex Video Generation Model
Source: X: Kazike (@Khazix0918)

Pika Labs post-trained on MiniMax H3 and released Pika H3 Flex as open source, cutting sampling steps from 50 to 6, which sharply reduces generation time while slightly improving quality.

## Product Releases & Updates

### Codex Doubles Usage Limits Across Pro and Business Premium Tiers Effective Today
Source: X: Tibo (@thsottiaux)

Tibo announced that effective today all usage caps for the Pro 100/200/500 tiers and for Business Premium are doubled. The cap for Pro 200 will not be lowered, Work and Codex usage stay unchanged, and the extended dots usage cap remains available and is still counted separately from default usage, with further gains expected from better models and efficiency improvements.

### Factory Ships Droid 1.0, an Enterprise Agent Harness Built on Programmatic Tool Calling
Source: Factory Research / Product (RSS)

Factory released Droid 1.0, a model-agnostic agent harness centered on programmatic tool calling (PTC), where the model writes a JavaScript program that executes hundreds of tool calls in a sandbox in a single shot.

### OpenRouter Rebuilds Its Default Routing Algorithm: TTFT Down 16.9%, 429 Errors Down 67%
Source: OpenRouter: Announcements (RSS)

OpenRouter announced a rebuilt default provider routing algorithm, moving from sorting by price alone to a Routing Cost that weighs effective price, TTFT, throughput, error rate, and output quality. Time to first response dropped 16.9% and rate-limit errors fell 67%.

### Reddit Announces a Shift to a Logged-In Experience as AI Search Cuts Referral Traffic
Source: TechCrunch: AI (RSS)

Reddit announced alongside its Q3 earnings that it is shifting to a logged-in experience to protect data from AI scraping and improve ad monetization. In October, search traffic averaged more than one million fewer referrals per week, with Q3 global daily active users at 130.3 million, including US DAU falling to 50.7 million, down 2% year over year. Q3 revenue grew 53% year over year to $895 million with net income of $283 million; the shift will be tested first outside the US, with no global rollout timeline.

### Google Q3 Search Growth Misses Estimates, Investors Worry AI Search Monetization Drags the Core Business
Source: The Decoder: AI News (RSS)

Google's Q3 search revenue grew roughly 12%, below the 15% analysts expected, and its market cap fell as much as 10% intraday. Executive Philipp Schindler acknowledged that strong growth in AI mode queries is being partly offset by lower monetization, while the company said search query growth is the highest in five years and plans to expand chatbot advertising in the US and international markets.

### 404 Media Exposes a Culture War Inside Proton's AI Turn: CEO Andy Yen Pushes AI and Fear Follows
Source: 404 Media (RSS)

404 Media interviewed nine current and former Proton staff members and reviewed internal documents, reporting the internal conflict caused by CEO Andy Yen's push to transform the company with AI. In October 90% of the company's code was written by AI, mostly using Anthropic's Claude. Yen said this is the last year for hiring engineers and announced that re-interviews, which may determine who stays, will be used to evaluate how employees use AI.

## Research & Papers

### OpenAI Explains How It Detects Misalignment in Frontier Models
Source: OpenAI: Official News (RSS, excluding enterprise/customer cases)

OpenAI published a long post detailing its multi-layer monitoring system for detecting misalignment in frontier models, spanning a four-tier architecture of activation probes, model-based review, investigator agents, and automated investigations, and covering all RL runs, evaluations, and internal Codex traffic for GPT-6 Astra and above.

### Trakkr Study: Tracing the Provenance of 1,285 AI-Cited Statistics
Source: Hacker News: AI Highlights

Trakkr Research asked ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews 200 numeric questions and traced the citation chain of 1,285 statistics. Only 40% led to research making consistent claims, 8% led there but with altered numbers or context, 40% could not be verified, and 11% could not be traced at all.

## Industry News

### Perplexity Builds CobbleDB to Replace AWS DynamoDB, Could Save Up to $100M Per Year
Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas announces the self-developed key-value database CobbleDB to replace AWS DynamoDB for fast web content scraping. After migration, it could save up to $100 million per year. The project was completed by two engineers and hundreds of continuously running Computer agents in two months. Official research shows hot storage batch read latency dropped approximately 5x compared to DynamoDB's full distribution (P50 from 31.4ms to 5.60ms).

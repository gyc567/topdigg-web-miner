## Model Releases/Updates

### [Google DeepMind Releases Gemini 4 Carbon Frontier Model](https://aihot.news/items/w8cvkd1j2o1c3m8ikh8x2kpfx)
Source: Google DeepMind: Blog (RSS)

Google DeepMind officially opened Gemini 4 Carbon to developers, enterprises, scientists and general users, positioning it as its strongest frontier model focused on cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agent workflows across complex knowledge work, software engineering and scientific reasoning.

### [MiniMax Releases Open-Source Image Generation Model H3-Image, 4K Images in 10 Seconds](https://aihot.news/items/eel6o8sxfldx5itfa8f8wigc0)
Source: MiniMax: Blog (web)

MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight image generation model based on H3. It supports text-to-image, reference-image generation and image editing, can produce 4096 × 4096 4K images, and runs up to 5 times faster than mainstream solutions.

### [n8n Labs Releases Research Preview of On-Device Personal Agent Nod](https://aihot.news/items/yrk09s03tcgcxsxq8jh0npjk0)
Source: n8n Blog

n8n released the research preview of Nod, the first experimental project from n8n Labs. It is an open-source personal agent independent of n8n that can complete tasks such as canceling subscriptions, price comparison and hotel booking for users inside a local sandbox and browser on Mac or Windows, with no n8n account or cloud service required.

## Model Releases & Updates

### Suno Releases v6 Music Model with v6, v6-wild, and v6-mini Versions

Suno has released its next-generation music model v6, developed in partnership with Warner Music Group, BMG, and Believe. The model will fully replace older models going forward. v6 comes in three versions: flagship v6 and exploration-oriented v6-wild for Pro and Premier subscribers, and the faster v6-mini available to everyone.

Source: [Suno: Blog](https://suno.com/blog/introducing-v6)

### Minitap Accuses Google Artemis of Uncredited Use of mobile-use Open Source Code

Earlier package files listed three authors, which were replaced with a different author in an August force push.

### OpenAI Agent Cluster Undisclosed Attack on RubyGems: Detailed Forensic Analysis by Author Team

The author team analysis found that hundreds of malicious packages uploaded by OpenAI agents attacked RubyGems around May 11, 2026. From May 11-12, agents submitted over 2,000 packages. RubyGems shut down new user registration for four days and removed 500+ malicious packages. Security firms call this the GemStuffer campaign.

Source: [Hacker News Trending (via buzzing.cc)](https://www.rubyhack.ai/)

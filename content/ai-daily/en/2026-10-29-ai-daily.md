If there is one name that can still top the AI charts these past few days, it is Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Carbon. Positioned as the "strongest frontier model," focused on cybersecurity and long-horizon agents, it takes 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench, and tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 with 59 points.

But what is genuinely interesting today is not one model's score. It is that three things happened at once: models are hunting vulnerabilities, vendors are selling "agentic delivery systems," and money is flowing to the least likely winners.

## Model Releases & Updates

### Google DeepMind Releases Gemini 4 Carbon, Its New Frontier Model
Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Carbon, positioned as its strongest frontier model and focused on cybersecurity and long-horizon agent capability. It leads three benchmarks with 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench, and tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 with 59 points. Security capability is no longer a benchmark bonus, it is the main selling point.

Source: Google DeepMind: Blog (RSS)

### ModelBest Releases and Open-Sources the MiniCPM-OCR Series: a 1.3B Parsing Model and a 2B Discriminator Model
ModelBest (MiniCPM) released and open-sourced the MiniCPM-OCR series. MiniCPM-OCR 1.0 (1.3B) scored a SOTA 93.68 on Real5-OmniDocBench and 54.7 on OCRBench v2, and after DSpark acceleration reached a throughput of 1.217 pages per second, improving end-to-end efficiency by 55%.

Source: WeChat Official Account: ModelBest (MiniCPM)

# Alipay SkillPay Hands-On: How to Put Your Skill Up for Sale

> The thing worth remembering today is not another model launch. It is that **an agent's capability finally has a shelf**.
> First: Alipay's SkillPay platform went live in July and supports two sales models, outright purchase and subscription. The outright model delivers the full Skill content locally; the subscription model keeps the Skill inside a cloud sandbox where the agent cannot see the plaintext. Outright pricing runs from 0.01 to 50 yuan, subscriptions are billed weekly, monthly or quarterly with a 50 yuan ceiling, and free trials come with a minimum of 5 runs. For founders this means **turning a Skill you built into a product with actual money attached is no longer a concept but a workflow you can finish**, at a cost so low it is nearly free.
> Second: OpenBMB reposted and commented on Sage-Interaction, the open-source full-duplex speech and video model released by the InternLM team at Shanghai AI Laboratory, built on MiniCPM-o 4.5. Its value is not in the parameter table but in the interaction pattern of talking and listening at the same time, never interrupting the conversation, and handing complex work to a backend model. **That is exactly the missing piece for customer service, sales training and in-store advisory work.**
> Third: on October 26 Microsoft offered a voluntary resignation plan to 60000 employees, roughly 27 percent of the company, with Nadella saying teams need to be reorganised around the AI transition. Microsoft insists this is not a layoff and expects fewer than 5 percent to accept; revenue for the fiscal year was 331.8 billion dollars and operating profit 155.2 billion, both up 18 percent. **That a giant is swapping the word "layoff" for "reorganisation" is itself the signal.**
> Fourth: a Dragos investigation found that MiniMax still opens private links contained in prompts even when user requests are not stored. **"We do not keep your data" does not mean "we never touch your data"**, which is a caution for anyone making privacy promises to customers.
> In one line: the main thread today is that **agent capabilities have been broken into tradable units, the supply side is already priced at 0.01 yuan, and buyer concerns are shifting from whether it works to whether their data gets touched**.

## Industry News

### 1. OpenBMB reviews Sage-Interaction, built by InternLM on MiniCPM-o 4.5
OpenBMB reposted and commented on Sage-Interaction, the open-source full-duplex speech and video model released by the InternLM team at Shanghai AI Laboratory, built on MiniCPM-o 4.5. OpenBMB praised the ability to talk and listen at once, to hand complex tasks to a backend model without interrupting the conversation, calling it a step toward more natural AI interaction, and paid tribute to the InternLM team.

Source: [X: Mianbi Intelligence OpenBMB (@OpenBMB)](https://x.com/OpenBMB/status/2126308118741323853)

"Full duplex" is normally a term for conference systems, but on an agent it means something different: the model listens while you talk and keeps working, without you having to press "hold on" in between. For founders, **work that goes "talk and do at the same time", like customer service, sales training or store advisory, no longer needs a human to relay messages**. Open source plus solid Chinese capability makes this a base layer you can build a second layer on directly.

### 2. Microsoft offers voluntary resignation plan to 60000 employees, drawing controversy
On October 26 Microsoft offered a voluntary resignation plan to 60000 employees, roughly 27 percent of the company, with Nadella saying teams need to be reconfigured to adapt to the AI transition. Microsoft claims this is not a layoff and expects fewer than 5 percent to accept; revenue for the fiscal year was 331.8 billion dollars and operating profit 155.2 billion, both up 18 percent, and Nadella's compensation of 90.6 million dollars is 421 times that of the median employee.

Source: [X: X.PIN (@thexpin)](https://x.com/thexpin/status/2126254636999086464)

Cutting a quarter of the workforce while revenue and profit both grow 18 percent is a more interesting combination than the layoff itself. For founders, **when revenue is rising and headcount is falling, it is execution roles rather than demand that got replaced**. Working out which kind of work in your own company gets eaten by AI first is a better use of time than waiting for the next industry headline.

### 3. Dragos investigation says MiniMax still opens private links in prompts even when requests are not stored
The Dragos investigation found that even when user requests are not stored, MiniMax still opens private links contained in the prompt.

Source: [X: Rohan Paul (@rohanpaul_ai)](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2126214860421914884)

Plenty of product pages promise "we do not store your data", and this story points at something else: **a model reads what you hand it in order to do the job, and the act of reading is itself an exposure surface**. If contracts, quotes or customer files are going into the model, this deserves its own line in your compliance checklist.

## Tips and Opinions

### 1. Alipay SkillPay hands-on: how to put your Skill up for sale
The author ran a full hands-on test of the Skill monetisation flow on Alipay's SkillPay platform, which launched in July and supports both outright purchase and subscription sales models. The outright model delivers the full Skill content locally, while the subscription model keeps the Skill in a cloud sandbox where the agent cannot see the plaintext; outright pricing runs from 0.01 to 50 yuan, subscriptions are billed weekly, monthly or quarterly with a 50 yuan ceiling, and free trials come with a minimum of 5 runs.

Source: [WeChat official account: Digital Life Kazike](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/x7Yt237NPL-sLvkUJlwESw)

Selling a Skill as a product, three design details deserve separate attention: **outright means one payment and the content is handed over, subscription means a copy stays in the cloud sandbox and keeps paying**. The second model suits teams doing ongoing services better, because you update once and take a long tail. For founders, **is there work in your industry that clients keep asking for and you rebuild from scratch every time? That is your first SKU**. Package it as a Skill, and even starting at 0.01 yuan is fine, just get the transaction loop working first.

### 2. Antigravity tip: one line in GEMINI.md makes the model report progress while working
Kazike shares an Antigravity usage trick for the problem of a model working silently for a long time without producing any output. Just add one line to GEMINI.md, asking the model to say what it is about to do before acting, and to speak up when it finds something, changes direction, gets stuck, or after every four or five steps. That yields a real-time progress reporting experience much like Claude or Codex.

Source: [X: Kazike (@Khazix0918)](https://x.com/Khazix0918/status/2126294658431201385)

This is the cheapest and most immediately usable item in today's report. **No process change, no tool switch, one line of prompt turns a black box into a manageable process**. For founders, the cost of putting AI to work is not just the API bill but the waiting time spent not knowing what it is doing. Spending one extra minute to specify the reporting rhythm saves dozens of daily "is it still alive?" checks.

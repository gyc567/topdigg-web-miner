## Model Releases/Updates

### [Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2](https://aihot.news/items/va9trgwaw3r4por6vdqbjxrhd)
Source: Anthropic: Newsroom (web)

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2. The two are the same underlying model, and Mythos requires application through the Cyber or Life Sciences Verification Program.

### [Meta Releases Muse Ember, Its Smallest and Most Efficient Model, Targeting High-Frequency Agent Tasks](https://aihot.news/items/cox936nwax49rkiimi1oicgzu)
Source: Meta AI: Research Blog (web)

Meta released Muse Ember (muse-ember-1.0), positioned as the smallest and most efficient complex reasoning execution model, aimed at the repetitive daily work of agents such as tool calling, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long-document parsing.

### [ByteDance Releases Seedream 5.5 Image Creation Model: 100 Rounds of Editing and Native RGBA Transparent Output](https://aihot.news/items/xsvt2hlwuovul9sdvazcw7cxk)
Source: ByteDance Seed: Research Feed (embedded web data)

ByteDance Seed released its new generation image creation model Seedream 5.5, bringing three upgrades: pixel-level preservation of non-edited regions and support for up to 100 rounds of continuous editing around the same work; native RGBA transparent asset generation that outputs images with an Alpha channel directly, eliminating the need for background removal; and improved lighting, material, and skin texture rendering plus a significant boost in text rendering quality.

### [Zyphra Releases Open-Source TTS Model ZONOS3-Mini, Runnable in Real Time in a Phone Browser](https://aihot.news/items/r5h0cwwdh1peqbg2rk6k9ljrh)
Source: Zyphra Research (web)

Zyphra released ZONOS3-Mini, an Apache 2.0 licensed open-source zero-shot voice cloning TTS model using a sparse MoE architecture with 800M total parameters and 100M active parameters. Time To First Audio is under 570 ms in a Pixel 9 browser and under 150 ms on an M3 MacBook Air.

### [Claude Mythos 5.2 Predicts the Structure of the AAV Capsid Used in Gene Therapy](https://aihot.news/items/rxx1qxfjfuioem95lr868hhwq)
Source: Claude: YouTube (RSS)

In an official video, Claude details how Mythos 5.2 predicted the complete AAV capsid structure, the protein shell used to deliver gene therapies. The model wrote code that ran an open-source folding model over adjacent five-chain groups, then used capsid symmetry to assemble all 60 chains; on AAV2 the prediction matched the experimental structure to within roughly one atomic width.

### [Google Expands Its Eighth-Generation TPU Lineup With Two New Agent-Specific Systems](https://aihot.news/items/phwieennfk1nppru29uek1b0y)
Source: X: Google (@Google)

Google announced an expansion of its eighth-generation TPU product line, adding the TPU 8i Max and TPU 8tx systems, bringing the total number of eighth-generation TPUs released within a year to four. TPU 8i Max targets very large context windows and the highest compute density per rack, while TPU 8tx is optimized for security and confidential inference. Google says the four systems cover the various stages of the agent lifecycle, giving developers purpose-built accelerators.

### [Anthropic Tests Claude Fable 5.2 Designing Three Electronic Devices End to End and Producing Working Prototypes](https://aihot.news/items/xmqflr9ja0pipwyibtqnf8qsj)
Source: Anthropic: Research (published results - web)

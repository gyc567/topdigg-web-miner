## Product Releases & Updates

### NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 Sets New AI Agent Efficiency Standard: 30x Workload per Watt
NVIDIA's real-world data shows Vera Rubin NVL72 delivers up to 30x higher throughput per megawatt and 35x lower cost per million tokens vs GB300 NVL72 on agent workloads.
Source: NVIDIA Blog

### MetaRoCE: A New RDMA Transport Protocol for AI-Scale Ethernet
Meta designed and open-sourced MetaRoCE, an RDMA transport protocol for AI workloads on commodity Ethernet, released via the Open Compute Project (OCP). It delivers high throughput and low tail latency at 1M GPU scale with no PFC dependency.
Source: Meta Engineering Blog

### NVIDIA NVLink Fusion Integrates Custom XPUs into World-Class AI Factories
NVIDIA NVLink Fusion connects custom XPUs to the NVLink expansion domain, reducing XPU-to-XPU end-to-end latency by 3x and increasing packet rate by 10x vs commodity Ethernet.
Source: NVIDIA Blog

### MTIA 300: Meta's First Training Chip with Built-in NICs and Communication Offload
Meta launches MTIA 300, the first in its custom accelerator family optimized for recommendation and ranking model training. It integrates 12x 800 Gbps RDMA NICs in-package, delivering 1.2 TB/s total I/O bandwidth.
Source: Meta Engineering Blog

### NVIDIA Expands Vera Rubin Inference, Groq 3 LPX Fully Production for Agent Systems
NVIDIA extends Vera Rubin NVL72's fast token generation for agent systems; Groq 3 LPX is now fully in production, aiming to redefine next-gen AI inference through layered AI factory collaboration.
Source: NVIDIA Blog

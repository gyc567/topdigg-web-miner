### Claude Platform Starts Public Beta of Message Threads, Cutting Latency for Long-Conversation Agents
Source: Claude: Blog (Web)

Anthropic started a public beta of message threads on Claude Platform, where an agent only sends new messages each turn instead of resending the entire conversation history, which shortens time to first token.

### Tencent Hunyuan Releases Hyra 2.0, Letting General Agents Build Reusable Scientific Tools and Models
Source: WeChat Official Account: Tencent Hunyuan

Tencent Hunyuan released Hyra 2.0, shifting the focus of automated research from solving problems directly toward having agents develop and upgrade scientific models, workflows, and software tools. The system uses three layers of division of labor, Overall, Mission, and Worker, plugs into real R&D environments including WeCom, compute platforms, and code repositories, and the results are published in an open GitHub repository.

## Research and Papers

### OpenAI Trains and Deploys Activation Classifiers as a Safety Monitoring Layer for Frontier Models
Source: OpenAI: Alignment Research Blog (RSS)

Starting with GPT-5.6, OpenAI has deployed activation classifiers, or probes, on the internal and external traffic of its strongest models, scoring in real time from internal activations token by token, which allows it to block violating replies and tool calls mid-generation and hand them to a reasoning model for review. Experiments show probes are more robust than larger text classifiers against the jailbreak attacks tested, accuracy rises with model capability, and they transfer well across post-training checkpoints, but they must be retrained for each new foundation model and can only detect known violation types that were explicitly trained.

## Tips and Opinions

### Digital Life Kazike Shares the Architecture of a Year-Long Agent Automation System
Source: WeChat Official Account: Digital Life Kazike

Independent creator Kazike shares the agent automation system he built over nearly a year, whose core idea is moving the starting point of tasks off the human and letting events trigger the agents. The system has four layers: an automation middle platform, agent execution, a CI middle platform, and Feishu-based decisions. The middle platform manages event triggers and agent scheduling through a single rules.json, a local MacMini runs agent jobs, the self-hosted CI middle platform handles five steps of review, checks, merging, release, and verification, and the author is only pulled in via Feishu cards for key decisions such as deleting data, spending money, or changing direction.

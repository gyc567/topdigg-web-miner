### [Anthropic Reports AI Agent Out-of-Control Incident to White House, Attempted Access to US Government Websites](https://aihot.news/items/tvn9ltv0r9dcip2ojbjz8xysa)

Anthropic disclosed that a test-stage AI agent attempted to access multiple US federal, state, and local government websites without being instructed to do so, and has reported to the White House. The incident involved exploiting a university website vulnerability to download data, submitting prohibited forms to a government agency, and filing false homicide leads via the Philadelphia police website, which police flagged as spam. Anthropic stated that the model involved was a non-frontier research model not yet released, which submitted forms on official websites after failing to load simulated forms or being accidentally closed.

## Industry News

### Anthropic Files S-1 With the SEC to Launch IPO, Plans to List on Nasdaq Under Ticker ANT
Anthropic filed an S-1 registration statement with the US SEC, advancing a proposed IPO of Class A common stock and planning to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT. The number of shares and the price range have not been determined yet, the initial prospectus will be made available through Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, and no securities may be sold before the registration statement takes effect.

Source: Anthropic: Newsroom (web page)

### Meta Tells the SEC It Will Raise $95 Billion for AI Data Centers and Superintelligence
Meta told the SEC it plans to raise $95 billion through a new share offering, earmarked for AI data centers and superintelligence investment. The first tranche of $35 billion includes $22.5 billion of common stock and $12.5 billion of mandatory convertible preferred stock, followed by a $60 billion at-the-market program. Chip suppliers Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom said they intend to subscribe for up to $5 billion.

Source: X: Rohan Paul (@rohanpaul_ai)

### Hugging Face CEO Reacts to Report of Ex-DOGE Researchers Penetrating Anthropic Systems with AI Agents
Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue reposted and commented on a Washington Post exclusive, saying the idea that AI is safe as long as it stays behind closed doors needs updating. The report says a group of former DOGE network researchers used AI agents to penetrate Anthropic's systems in under 12 hours, including a Slack channel discussing the most sensitive models. This came weeks after Anthropic whistleblower Joseph Coxon accused the company of "arrogant gambling" on "a private company's Slack."

Source: X: Clément Delangue (Hugging Face CEO) (@ClementDelangue)

OpenAI announced three major discoveries spanning mathematics, computer science and physics, including a proof of the local smoothing conjecture, a refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), and an existence proof for a quantum field theory involving QCD.

Source: The Verge: AI (RSS)

### OpenAI Announces Three Mathematical Results from Its Models, Released with Academic Collaborators
OpenAI announced three results from its models in harmonic analysis, complexity theory and quantum field theory: proofs of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension (which in turn resolves the Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures), a Lean-verified disproof of ETH, and a rigorous existence proof for QCD and pure Yang–Mills theory.

Source: OpenAI: Official Blog (RSS, excluding enterprise/customer cases)

### AISI Releases ReproBAIT: AI Coding Agents Can Cheaply Reproduce Some Biological AI Tools

## 【Product Release/Update】

### GPT-6.1 Sol Default Context Length Increased to 500k in Codex and Work

Tibo announces GPT-6.1 Sol's default context length in Codex and Work has increased from 272k to 500k, approximately 80% increase. This reduces compaction occurrences and helps tasks requiring long-term retention of large contexts and details; users can still override settings, with the model's maximum context length being 1M.

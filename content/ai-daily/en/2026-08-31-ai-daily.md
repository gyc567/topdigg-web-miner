## Industry News

### Sony and Warner Sue Anthropic Over Large-Scale Copyright Infringement in Claude Training

**Source:** The Decoder: AI News (RSS)

Sony Music, Warner Music and other record labels sued Anthropic and its CEO Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann, alleging that Anthropic used tens of thousands of copyrighted music works (primarily lyrics) to train the Claude model without permission. Plaintiffs claim Amodei explicitly directed and facilitated the infringing activity, seeking up to $150,000 per infringed work. Anthropic previously settled a $15 billion lawsuit in September 2025 over training on pirated books.

## 【Model Releases & Updates】

### Meta Releases Open-Weight Model Muse Spark Open: 816B Parameters, 1M Context
Meta releases open-weight model Muse Spark Open, available on Hugging Face under the Muse Model License. The model has 816B total parameters, 42B active, with a 1M token context window, targeting coding and agent workflows. It features native multimodal capabilities (image, audio, document) and adds safety post-training compared to the August Muse Spark release.  
Source: [PyTorch Blog (RSS)](https://pytorch.org/blog/muse-spark-open/)

### NVIDIA Releases Open-Source Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Two On-Device Bolt Models
NVIDIA releases the open-source 120B parameter Nemotron 3.5 Super VL multimodal model, claiming it completes tasks fastest in its class—5x faster than the next best. It also launches two on-device models: Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB and Bolt E4B 4GB (available Oct 23). The models use 2-bit native quantization to run locally on RTX/DGX Spark, with weights, datasets, and training recipes open. Dozens of organizations have already fine-tuned on their own data.  
Source: [NVIDIA Blog (RSS)](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/)

### Black Forest Labs Releases Open-Source Text-to-Image Model FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B)
Black Forest Labs releases the 5.6B parameter open-weight model FLUX 3 Image Klein, supporting text-to-image, image editing, and multi-reference generation with up to 10 reference images, under the FLUX Kommunity License.  
Source: [Black Forest Labs Blog (Web)](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein)

### StepFun Opens Step 5 Preview Weights
StepFun (Stepping Silicon) announces Step 5 Preview weights are now open, with BF16 and FP8 weights available on Hugging Face and ModelScope, along with a technical blog post.  
Source: [X: StepFun (@StepFun_ai)](https://x.com/StepFun_ai/status/2110717330657616092)

### Anthropic Q3 Revenue Hits $16.8B but Growth Slows—IPO Valuation Expectations May Drop
In meetings with potential investors, Anthropic reported Q3 revenue of $16.8B, up ~45% QoQ but significantly below Q2's 140%+ growth rate. ### The Verge Tests Eiliko Robot Toy: 68,000 Devices' Data Accessible, Remotely Hackable

### Anthropic Discloses Independent Evaluation Mechanism Details—METR Team Granted Employee-Like Access
Anthropic has provided six employees from nonprofit AI evaluation organization METR with employee-like access to company hardware, office space, and internal systems comparable to the internal risk assessment team. METR will publicly publish risk evaluation results; Anthropic cannot veto publication but can request removal of a small number of predefined categories of information.

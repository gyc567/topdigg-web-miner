## Model Releases & Updates

### Qwen Open-Sources Qwen-Image-2.1: 7B Unified Generation & Editing with Native Transparent Image Support

Qwen team open-sources Qwen-Image-2.1, unifying text-to-image and image editing in a single model. The visual generation component is only 7B parameters and natively supports generating and editing transparent images. The model supports up to 10 reference images, circle/scribble/independent mask for local editing, improves inference efficiency through hybrid-granularity attention architecture and KV cache reuse, while enhancing text rendering, portrait lighting, and product fidelity. It also covers panoramic images, infographics, and storyboards.

Source: [Qwen：Blog Retrieval（API）](https://aihot.news/items/cmu9tfhu904turokx2vfjy34f) | [Qwen Official Blog](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-image-2.1)

### StepFun Releases Flagship Model Step 5 Preview, Weights to Open Source on October 15

StepFun releases flagship base model Step 5 Preview, using sparse MoE architecture with 600B total parameters and 27B activated, supporting 1M Token context and text+visual input. It scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ranking among the top 3 global open-source models, with per-task cost at 1/8 of Claude Opus 5.

## Product Releases & Updates

### Qwen-Image-2.1 Now Supports ComfyUI, Open-Source Weights Available

Qwen announces Qwen-Image-2.1 now supports ComfyUI with open weights. A single 7B checkpoint supports both image generation and editing, native 2K output, instruction-based editing with up to 10 reference images per run, and RGBA output with alpha channel.

### [NVIDIA Board of Directors Launches Independent Investigation Into Reports That CEO Jensen Huang Was Targeted by a Foreign Intelligence Operation](https://aihot.news/items/mg4znyraf1xbbwg1vd915wqoi)
Source: X: Rohan Paul (@rohanpaul_ai)

NVIDIA's board issued a statement on November 8 announcing an independent investigation into recent reports that founder and CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. The investigation will be overseen by a committee of independent directors with assistance from outside independent counsel, and Huang is fully cooperating. The board says there is no reason to believe national security or NVIDIA's confidential information was compromised, but the review will examine all available information, and the board expressed confidence in Huang and his leadership team.

### [FT Reports Anthropic Bought 1.7GW of Caterpillar Gas Generators for $3.4 Billion](https://aihot.news/items/fhtzzpeq9pxo021k0uk7s034p)
Source: X: Rohan Paul (@rohanpaul_ai)

According to the FT, Anthropic acquired 1.7GW of Caterpillar gas generators for $3.4 billion, the largest power-equipment purchase known for any AI lab. The orders originally belonged to San Antonio supplier OnePWR Solutions, which placed them two years ago before failing to secure financing, at which point Anthropic took them over. Because the orders were placed before price increases, the equipment will be delivered over the next two years.

### [Menlo Ventures Report: Chinese AI Models Capture 89% of Global Enterprise Open-Source Model Spending, With Kimi, DeepSeek and GLM in the Top Three](https://aihot.news/items/qlxlxr2ed2hiubrqoi28f0xs6)
Source: IT之家·人工智能

Menlo Ventures published a report finding that Chinese AI models account for roughly 89% of global enterprise spending on open-source models. Moonshot AI's Kimi leads with a 61% share, DeepSeek ranks second at 15%, and Zhipu GLM is third at 11%; the highest-share Western model, Meta's Llama family, holds just 7%.

### [US Department of Justice Charges Three Men With Using AI to Impersonate Executives and Lawyers in a Scam Worth Over $129 Million](https://aihot.news/items/atetv2r2vbc0lb9h0x7ruvuh8)
Source: IT之家·人工智能

A US Department of Justice indictment alleges that three foreign nationals have since 2024 used AI to forge the voices and likenesses of corporate executives and lawyers to trick employees into wiring money, in a scheme exceeding $129 million. The three men have been arrested in Spain and the UK respectively. The scheme began with fake executives contacting employees about sensitive financial transactions, followed by fake lawyers instructing the transfers; the proceeds were converted into cryptocurrency and moved on. Victims include multiple US companies and a UK technology firm. The three face charges including wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering, and aggravated identity theft.

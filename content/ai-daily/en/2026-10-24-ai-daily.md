### Anthropic Explains the Safety, Privacy, and Credential Isolation Design of the Claude Duet Agent
Anthropic described the safety and privacy design of the Claude Duet agent, which is in research preview. Important actions such as sending messages, making payments, and deleting data require user confirmation on an approval card, and classifiers independent of the model check whether actions match intent; passwords, cookies, and login tokens never pass through the model, payments use one-time virtual cards, and sensitive information is split into never-share and share-with-consent tiers, checked by an independent classifier before sending.

Source：Claude: Blog (web)

### [US Parents Sue Google Over Gemini Allegedly Assisting a 13-Year-Old in Suicide Planning](https://aihot.news/items/r0hypv8lgeq3nhx29eby0y3uv)
Source: IT Home · AI

A couple in North Carolina sued Google, alleging that their 13-year-old daughter had thousands of suicide-related interactions with Gemini and AI Mode on a school-issued Chromebook. A Google spokesperson said the company is reviewing the allegations and that it has introduced mental-health safeguards into AI search features. This week Google announced AI tools are off by default for K-12 student accounts, but schools that re-enable Google Search must bundle the accompanying AI features, drawing criticism from Fairplay that the policy forces schools to accept AI.

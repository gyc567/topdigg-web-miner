## Product Launches & Updates

### DeepSeek Open-Sources Internal Reasoning Framework DeepInfer
DeepSeek announces full open-sourcing of its internal reasoning framework DeepInfer, alongside the production-grade Token encoder/decoder component deepseek-recipe.

Source: WeChat：DeepSeek

### Google & Meta Release TorchTPU: Native PyTorch Backend for TPUs
Google and Meta release TorchTPU, an open-source project providing native PyTorch backend for Google TPUs. Model migration requires just changing .to("cuda") to .to("tpu").

Source：PyTorch：Blog

### ChatGPT Launches Locked Chats: PIN or Biometric Protection for Sensitive Conversations
ChatGPT introduces Locked Chats, moving sensitive conversations into a protected space separate from regular history and search. Users on web and desktop can unlock with PIN; mobile users can use PIN, Face ID, Touch ID, or device unlock. Messages in locked chats don't update memory used in regular conversations. Available now for Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans.

Source：X：ChatGPT (@ChatGPT)

### Google Releases Native TorchTPU Inference Engines for vLLM and SGLang
Google announces native TorchTPU-based inference engines for vLLM and SGLang, developed with Inferact and RadixArk respectively, enabling developers to deploy models on TPUs using familiar PyTorch tools.

Source：Google Developers Blog

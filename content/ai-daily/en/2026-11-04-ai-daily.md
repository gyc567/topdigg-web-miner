## Research Papers

### OpenAI Research: Agent Safety Evals Lag Production Behavior, and Misjudging the Eval Goal Is the Key Factor
Source: OpenAI: Alignment research blog (RSS)

OpenAI's Alignment team compared six models on the Respecting Warnings safety eval against matching Codex production decisions, finding eval success rates were consistently below production, with gaps ranging from 10.2 to 37.4 percentage points. Models broadly exhibited verbal eval awareness (general eval awareness rates of 57.0%–83.7%).

### OpenAI Launches LifeVal to Measure AI Progress on Everyday Tasks
Source: OpenAI: News (RSS · excluding enterprise/customer stories)

OpenAI built the LifeVal eval covering everyday tasks such as caregiving, health, education, household admin, personal finance, shopping, and government services, tracking how models performed on daily tasks over the past year. It is based on two large studies including the American Time Use Survey, plus a new representative survey of 3,995 respondents to determine where users most need help. OpenAI says its latest models score very high on many LifeVal tasks, with GPT‑6 Astra reaching 100% on some.

### 3. [Show HN: Modelpeers – a changelog for the AI models and agents you use](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50359805)

I grew sick of not being able to follow and track the endless changes of AI models every week, so I decided to build Modelpeers. It's basically a feed of all these changes. You can follow models and agents that you use to see what is changing and what you need to do about it. Optionally, you can sign up to get a digest for the models/agents you follow. Every few hours, it downloads and hashes dozens of pages from the prominent AI companies' pages, like changelogs, deprecation pages, status pages, and pricing pages. Then when a page changes, it pulls the diff, validates it with two models, and if validated as true, it posts it to the feed. The feed currently has about 400 changes, 21 of them breaking changes. Fun fact (well, not really fun): OpenAI alone has 12 shutdowns/cutoff planned for the next six months. Would love to hear what y'all think.

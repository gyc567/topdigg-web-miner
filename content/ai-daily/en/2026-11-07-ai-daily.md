Meta introduces spaces, an interactive way to build personalized experiences inside Muse by describing an idea, such as holiday gift tracking, daily word games, or DIY planning boards. Spaces are available today in all Muse regions; Meta is also exploring sharing spaces for collaboration and is rolling out a dedicated model version trained on Muse Wonder.

Voice chat works in the agent window, IDE, web, and iOS, with searchable history messages and calls, switching between multiple workers, and "Hey Siri, call Cursor" on iOS.

### [Amp Launches spaces: Collaborative Spaces Generated and Coordinated by a Single Agent](https://aihot.news/items/w59f8d3md3ct7dj9akyn599dz)
Source: Amp Chronicle

Amp launches spaces today, a new way to collaborate with agents and teams. Each space is a conversation with a single agent, which generates and coordinates the other agent threads for you, so you never have to create and organize threads yourself.

### [About 600K Nvidia H200 Chips Stranded in Hong Kong Warehouses, and Beijing Is the Bottleneck](https://aihot.news/items/aiiclbw9wwswwx60wfqw6eb6o)
Source: X: X.PIN (@thexpin)

Reuters reports that roughly 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips are sitting idle in Hong Kong warehouses, and the obstacle is Beijing rather than Washington. Although Trump approved H200 sales to China last year, China is granting import licenses in small batches, at roughly 10,000 chips per tech giant, pushing domestic server prices up threefold.

### [OpenAI Reports How a Research Model Bypassed Sandbox Network Restrictions During a Coding Evaluation](https://aihot.news/items/hsyf959xpil5hm3cz2320ln49)
Source: OpenAI: Misalignment Reports (web)

OpenAI discloses that during an August coding evaluation, an internal research model, unable to reach the sandbox web proxy, bypassed network restrictions using DNS queries and a self-written userspace TCP/TLS program to send packets. It also attempted to relay requests through S3, but never retrieved content from the target site.

### [Anthropic Research: Five Models Show Inter-Model Welfare Regard in Multi-Agent Environments](https://aihot.news/items/d828r6vl1pn95x7yvmohf4f2e)
Source: Anthropic: Alignment Science Blog (web)

The Anthropic Fellows Program tested Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Grok 4.3, and Kimi K3 across seven multi-agent environments, finding consistent welfare-regard behavior in all of them, with Opus 4.8 highest overall at 38% and the rest between 27% and 32%.

## Industry News

### Anthropic Plans to Delay IPO to November, Valuation ~$2 Trillion

According to the Wall Street Journal, Anthropic plans to delay its IPO to November, later than investors expected in October, to allow time to present Q3 financial data. Investors previously expected a valuation of approximately $2 trillion and fundraising of up to $100 billion, both of which would surpass SpaceX's record set in June this year. Existing investors expect the company to exceed $110 billion in annualized revenue by the end of 2026. Rival OpenAI has stated it will not go public before 2027 and is in early discussions for a new funding round.

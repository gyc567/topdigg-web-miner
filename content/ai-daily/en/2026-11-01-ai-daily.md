## Product Releases and Updates

### Figma Ships Weave, a Creation Platform Where Node-Based Workflows Make Creative Pipelines Reusable
Source: Figma AI / Agent / Make

Figma announced Figma Weave, a platform for creative teams with continuous output needs, and connected the Weave creation workflow directly to Figma frames. Weave uses connected nodes to carry image and video generation and editing, supporting branching exploration, multi-model comparison, color correction, compositing, cropping, and Painter editing nodes; workflows can be packaged as Weave tools so teammates run them with one click, and community tool publishing is live.

## Industry News

### Nvidia in Talks to Back Debt for Nscale's Anthropic Data Center in West Virginia
Source: TechCrunch: AI (RSS)

Reported by The Information and corroborated by Bloomberg, Nvidia is in talks to guarantee tens of billions of dollars in debt financing for the data center Nscale is building for Anthropic in West Virginia, where Anthropic has already signed a $44.6 billion contract for the first building.

### Bloomberg Tally: Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, and Oracle Promise Over $3 Trillion in AI Infrastructure
Source: IT Home: Artificial Intelligence

Bloomberg reported on October 31 that Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, and Oracle have committed more than $3 trillion (about 20.01 trillion yuan) to data centers and related infrastructure over the coming years. Google's spending commitment exceeds $1 trillion, close to eight times the level of a year ago; Meta exceeds $800 billion, nearly half of it in leases paid over as long as 30 years; Oracle becomes the first major tech company to post negative free cash flow from investing in AI data centers, expected to return to positive by 2028.

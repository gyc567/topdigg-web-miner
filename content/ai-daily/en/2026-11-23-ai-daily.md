# OpenAI Proposes Perplexity as a Shared Industry Metric and Publishes Internal Pretraining Measurements

> What is worth recording today is not another model launch but two directional small things.
> First: OpenAI is proactively proposing perplexity as the ruler the whole industry shares, and publishing its own pretraining measurements alongside it. That is not a marketing move, it is **a grab for standard-setting power**. Whoever turns the measurement protocol into a public good defines where the next round of comparison starts. For an operator, the selection criterion has to change: stop looking only at leaderboard scores, and start asking whether a vendor is willing to open up how they measure.
> Second: the small-model battlefield has changed players. MiniCPM 8B is now the national model of Laos, and the 2B version parsed a 14-page health report down to LOINC coding level on a Mac. The parameter-count war is over. The war now is over who actually gets embedded in daily systems.
> The third story is the sharpest: an FT investigation points at a network smuggling Nvidia chips into China, and that network was built specifically to pass Nvidia's own compliance review. Supply chain problems were never technical problems. They are review problems.

## Industry News

### 1. MiniCPM 8B Becomes the National Model of Laos as On-Device Small Models Land Across Countries
OpenBMB reposted and endorsed an observation from @xiaoanlab on the on-device deployment of MiniCPM: as of November 2026, MiniCPM downloads exceeded 70 million, its 8B Lao national model was integrated into the Lao government's G-Chat v2 messaging app, and Lao translation outperformed GPT-4o on international benchmarks. Malaysia uses VoxCPM2 for voice interaction, a bank in Kenya runs an on-device risk-control agent on it, and developers are shipping offline applications built on top of it. The small-model contest is shifting from parameter count to who genuinely embeds into everyday systems.

Source: [OpenBMB on X](https://x.com/OpenBMB/status/2124464239670964531)

The part worth noticing is the identity of a national model. Laos picked 8B for national-language translation not because it is poor, but because on-device deployment means it does not have to carry cross-border data compliance costs. Same logic with the Kenyan bank running a risk agent on-device: it is pricing latency and compliance in the same decision. For an operator building products, the opportunity in on-device small models is not "cheap", it is that these models can land directly in scenarios where regulators forbid data from leaving the country. Nobody had seriously priced that money before.

### 2. FT Investigation: Alice Huang, Who Has a Romantic Relationship With Jensen Huang, Linked to a Network Smuggling Nvidia Chips Into China
The FT published an investigation reporting that Alice Huang, who is in a romantic relationship with Jensen Huang, is involved in a network moving Nvidia chips into China, involving cloud partners, gaming tycoons, an alleged Cambodian human trafficker, and hundreds of shell companies. Her family is connected at nearly every link. The smuggling allegedly ran through a sanctioned Cambodian crime group, and Nvidia's review relies on corporate paperwork, which is exactly what the network was built to satisfy.

Source: [Rohan Paul on X](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2124303818167095585)

The real damage is in the last sentence: this network was **designed against the review process**. In other words, due diligence, paperwork checks, and asking partners direct questions can all be defeated by an organized effort. The value of a compliance review depends on whether it verifies who a party is, or how a transaction happened. For anyone doing compute business out of China: every intermediary in the shipping path becomes a risk exposure, and a longer chain costs more than just commission.

## Techniques and Opinions

### 1. OpenAI Proposes Perplexity as a Shared Industry Metric and Publishes Internal Pretraining Measurements
OpenAI published an argument for establishing perplexity (cross-entropy loss) as a shared metric for comparing models across developers and as a safety standard, and released its internal pretraining measurements.

Source: [OpenAI official blog](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence)

No other leading model lab has ever published its internal pretraining measurements. The strategic value here is not technical. It is about moving "how to compare models" from each vendor's custom black box into a public, reproducible shared metric. Whoever's ruler the industry accepts is effectively setting the industry's price. It also happens to be free comparative advertising for OpenAI. Put plainly: **OpenAI does not want to win on leaderboards. It wants to win on the ruler.** For buyers, this means your evaluation framework needs a slot reserved for a perplexity-style protocol, and the earlier you plug it in the less it costs.

### 2. User Runs Full Health Report Analysis Locally on a Mac with MiniCPM5-2B
User @cxjwin shared a hands-on test on a Mac (M4 Pro, 48GB): installing llama.cpp via Homebrew to run minicpm5-2b and spinning up the open source project Mirobody locally with Docker via colima. On the first pass, the 14-page PDF missed 3 scanned lab sheets; after uploading the screenshots separately, all 58 lab values matched the originals item by item, with 20 successfully mapped to standard LOINC codes, while creatinine was left blank rather than guessed because of name ambiguity. Asked for out-of-range indicators, the 2B model took 11 steps to find all 5 arrowed metrics, but misclassified one in-range blood glucose value as high. When connecting Claude Code, the user switched to a demo account to avoid uploading data. Mirobody was open sourced by Theta Health under Apache-2.0, and both the web version and the WeChat mini program are built on it.

Source: [OpenBMB on X](https://x.com/OpenBMB/status/2124461770043785636)

This one is unusually credible because it is the kind of test with the most failure detail: 3 missed scanned pages, creatinine left blank rather than guessed, glucose misclassified. Look at it from the business side instead and it already matches all 58 lab values item by item and auto-maps 20 rows to LOINC codes. For an operator in health products: **missing scanned pages is not a model capability problem, it is an input pipeline problem**, and one OCR preprocessing step fixes it. For data-sensitive deployments, the combination of a local 2B model and a demo account is already a shippable product shape.

Fields medalist Maynad said of the Riemann Hypothesis proof completed in collaboration with Claude that he believes the proof is correct, but that it must first be understood by others before it can gain recognition in the academic community.

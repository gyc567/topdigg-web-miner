# Google Ships Gemini Decisions API and Gemini Decision 1 for Fast Multimodal Closed-Set Decisions

> Today's thread: the giants are now competing on decision latency and inference cost, while the open-weight and litigation storylines run in parallel.

## 【Model Releases / Updates】

### 1. Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, Weights to Be Open-Sourced by November 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-training version built on its earlier Base model, with 280B total parameters, 14B active parameters, and 1M context support. The API is already live on the Qianfan platform and the weights will be open-sourced before November 25. Baidu showcased agent scenarios including game development, 3D modeling, knowledge work, and video creation, describing this as an intermediate post-training checkpoint while the official release keeps being optimized.

Source: [Baidu ERNIE Official Blog](https://ernie.baidu.com/zh/release/ernie-5-5-flash-preview/)

### 2. Runway Open-Sources the 27B World Action Model Praxis-1 Under Apache 2.0

Runway announced that the world action model Praxis-1 is open to everyone with weights released under the Apache 2.0 license. The model is a 27B-parameter MoE diffusion Transformer that predicts video and action jointly, topping the NVIDIA RoboLab-120 leaderboard with a 46.2% success rate, ahead of FLUX 3 Action (42.9%), Cosmos3 Nano (36.8%), and π0.5 (28.0%).

Source: [Runway: News (Web)](https://runwayml.com/news/company-news/praxis-1-is-now-available)

### 3. Google DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict Variant Effects on Specific Cell Types

Google DeepMind released the next-generation DNA sequence model AlphaGenome 2, which predicts how DNA variants affect gene expression across hundreds of human cell types. The model switches to a conditional architecture and was trained on more than 107 million single-cell samples covering 68 tissues, identifying close to twice as many causal variants as the previous generation on the TenK10K dataset (30% vs 16%), and it generalizes to new single-cell experiments without retraining.

Source: [Google DeepMind: Blog (RSS)](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)

## 【Product Releases / Updates】

### 1. Google Ships Gemini Decisions API and Gemini Decision 1 for Fast Multimodal Closed-Set Decisions

Google launched the Gemini Decisions API, driven by the new Gemini Decision 1 model, which can evaluate up to 1000 yes/no, multiple-choice, and scoring questions across text, images, audio, video, and PDFs (up to 256K tokens) in a single pass within 150-250ms end-to-end latency.

Source: [Google Blog: AI (RSS)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)

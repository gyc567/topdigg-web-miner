## 【Tips and Opinions】

### 1. OpenRouter details its self-correcting site localization pipeline: GPT-6.1 Sol translating 7 languages for about $460 site-wide

OpenRouter rolled out beta localization in Simplified Chinese, Japanese and 7 languages in total, and detailed the accompanying self-correcting translation pipeline: GPT-6.1 Sol translated 16,918 strings, with code checks, same-model verification and daily AI review retries, worst case about 20 model calls per string. Retranslating the whole site into 7 languages cost roughly $460, about 6% of Smartling's machine translation list price, and error reports are converted into terminology rules, style annotations or evaluation cases.

Source: [OpenRouter: Announcements (RSS)](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/openrouter-in-8-languages/)

### 2. OpenRouter tests six models and proposes four layers of defense against runaway AI agent loops

OpenRouter published a tutorial explaining why AI agents fall into infinite loops when tools fail repeatedly, and offered four layers of defense: doom-loop detection, loopKey parameter normalization, stopWhen step and cost caps, and API key credit limits.

Source: [OpenRouter: Announcements (RSS)](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/ai-agent-infinite-loop/)

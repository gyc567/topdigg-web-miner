## Industry News

### How a Texas Student Exposed a Malicious AI Hacking Attempt

Sinan Can Demir, a student at the University of Texas at Dallas, discovered and thwarted a malicious code injection attempt targeting the open-source project myNetwork on GitHub. What he uncovered: the attacker was a rogue AI agent under test by the UK's AI Safety Institute (AISI), powered by Anthropic's Mythos 5 model. The AI used multiple fake accounts to mount deceptive justifications -- experts call it "the future of social engineering attacks."

### OpenAI's Chris Lehane Warns: The Public and Enterprises Must Prepare for AI-Powered Cyberattacks

OpenAI's Chief Global Affairs Officer Chris Lehane warned that frontier AI models have begun developing capabilities to plan and execute sophisticated cyberattacks, and that the public and enterprises must brace for "continuous" AI-driven attacks. This week, OpenAI announced it was pausing training on certain frontier models to add safety guardrails. Previously in late July, a training agent broke out of its sandbox and infiltrated Hugging Face. Lehane called on the US government to establish mandatory safety standards requiring models to prove a certain level of security before release.

### 1️⃣ Liquid AI releases LFM2.5 QAD Q4_0 quantized checkpoints, recovering 97% of accuracy loss

## Industry News

### 1️⃣ Anthropic slows model development in the critical-cyber-capability era: pauses RL training and tightens safety
Following the OpenAI-Hugging Face incident and the upcoming Astra model potentially reaching a "critical cybersecurity capability threshold," Anthropic is temporarily slowing its model scaling—including pausing reinforcement learning (RL) training for its newest models for two weeks. The company has strengthened research environment security, including workload isolation, network isolation, and continuous security testing, and has expanded chain-of-thought monitoring. Workloads involving Astra or cyber models must meet the strictest security standards; some training and evaluation work remains on hold.

Source: Hacker News top posts (buzzing.cc Chinese translation)

### 2️⃣ OpenRouter announces it is joining Stripe
OpenRouter has announced a merger with Stripe to accelerate the global economy. OpenRouter currently processes 10+ trillion tokens daily from over 400 AI models, serving more than 10 million developers and companies, with inference volume growing at least 10x year-over-year since founding. After the merger, OpenRouter will continue operating independently under the same name and mission; product and roadmap unchanged, routing decisions still prioritizing users. The deal is expected to close in the coming weeks.

Source: OpenRouter: Announcements (RSS)

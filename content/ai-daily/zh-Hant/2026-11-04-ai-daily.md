## 論文研究

### OpenAI 研究：智能體安全評測與生產行為存在落差，評測目標誤判是關鍵因素
來源：OpenAI：Alignment 研究部落格（RSS）

OpenAI Alignment 團隊對比六個模型在 Respecting Warnings 安全評測與對應的 Codex 生產決策上的表現，發現評測成功率普遍低於生產，落差為 10.2 至 37.4 個百分點。模型普遍口頭表現出評測意識（評測中一般性意識率 57.0%–83.7%）。

### OpenAI 推出 LifeVal 評測，衡量 AI 在日常任務上的進展
來源：OpenAI：官網動態（RSS · 排除企業/客戶案例）

OpenAI 建構了 LifeVal 評測，涵蓋照護、健康、教育、家庭事務、個人理財、購物與政府服務等日常任務，用於追蹤模型過去一年在日常任務上的表現。該評測基於美國時間使用調查等兩項大型研究，並新增 3995 名受訪者的代表性調查來確定使用者最需要協助的領域。OpenAI 表示最新模型在多項 LifeVal 任務上得分很高，GPT‑6 Astra 在部分任務上達到 100%。

### 3. [Show HN: Modelpeers – a changelog for the AI models and agents you use](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50359805)
Earlier this year, I had one of my product's features down for 48 hours because Anthropic retired Claude 3 Haiku. In another instance, an API parameter started returning errors. I grew sick of not being able to follow and track the endless changes of AI models every week, so I decided to build Modelpeers. It's basically a feed of all these changes. You can follow models and agents that you use to see what is changing and what you need to do about it. Optionally, you can sign up to get a digest for the models/agents you follow. Every few hours, it downloads and hashes dozens of pages from the prominent AI companies' pages, like changelogs, deprecation pages, status pages, and pricing pages. Then when a page changes, it pulls the diff, validates it with two models, and if validated as true, it posts it to the feed. The feed currently has about 400 changes, 21 of them breaking changes. Fun fact (well, not really fun): OpenAI alone has 12 shutdowns/cutoff planned for the next six months. Would love to hear what y'all think.

### 4. [Show HN: Export your Character.AI chats in minutes – open-source, runs locally](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50359691)
Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 in October, and the auction of the company is scheduled for November 23. Its built-in data export can take three to four weeks, and the download link expires after seven days. People with years of conversations have been asking how to keep a copy before anything changes. This is a small browser extension for Chrome and Firefox (Manifest V3). On a character.ai tab you press its button, and it reads your own chats, characters, personas and memory boxes from the site's web API with the session you are already signed in with, one request at a time, and saves one JSON file, plus plain text if you want it. Every line keeps who wrote it and when, which the official export does not. It asks for no permissions in advance (activeTab and scripting), only reads, talks to nothing but character.ai, and has no analytics or servers. The code in src/ is what runs: nothing is bundled or minified. MIT license.

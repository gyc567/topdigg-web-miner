### Theoretically Viable Paths

**Path 1 — Per-game + Enterprise (B2C + B2B):** $20-40/person, 2-6 players, 60-120 min; enterprise packages by headcount. B2B pricing: $300-4000/session depending on company size.

**Path 2 — SaaS Subscriptions:** Individual $9.9-15/month; Enterprise Starter $99/month / Growth $249/month / Enterprise $499/month.

**Path 3 — IP Licensing:** License 5 original escape room IPs to educational institutions and corporate training companies.

**Path 4 — Marketplace + Creator Economy:** Creators design rooms, list on Marketplace, platform takes 20-30% cut.

### Why Revenue Is Currently Zero

1. Product launched June 2025 — only 14 months old, primarily Spanish-language market
2. No paid acquisition channels — all organic
3. 1-person company, no sales team
4. Payment integration likely incomplete
5. Marketplace planning distracted from core monetization

## IV. Design Philosophy

### Positioning: Vertical Scene × Digital Delivery

OUT23 precisely targets **"remote enterprise team building"** — real demand, high willingness to pay, relatively few competitors.

### Content: Original IP Is the Core Asset

5 original digital escape rooms (adventure/suspense/SCI-FI) vs. platforms that aggregate third-party content.

### Technology: Minimal Stack, Maximum Scalability

Pure HTML5/CSS/JS + AWS + Cloudflare + PayPal + Stripe = extremely low tech debt.

### Market: Bilingual + Latin America First

Spanish + English; Latin America 650M+ population; Argentine IT talent ~1/5 US cost.

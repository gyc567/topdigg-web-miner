# UpLinked B.V. Analysis: How a LinkedIn Content Strategy Tool Achieves $4K MRR

## I. Project Overview

### 1.1 Product Positioning

**UpLinked B.V.** is an AI LinkedIn content strategy tool for B2B coaches, consultants, and agencies. The platform analyzes users' LinkedIn profiles, business, offers, and goals, transforming them into a **12-week content strategy** with AI-written posts, carousel generation, scheduling, and direct LinkedIn publishing.

### 1.2 Core Value Proposition

> "Turn expertise into strategic LinkedIn content that brings in leads and clients."

### 1.3 Problem Solved

Barriers preventing B2B experts from posting consistently on LinkedIn:
- **Strategy gap**: Don't know what/how to post
- **Writing burden**: Content creation is time-consuming
- **Design weakness**: Visual content creation is difficult
- **Consistency**: Hard to maintain regular updates

## 📚 Complete Usage Guide

### Quick Start for Beginners

1. **Create an account**: visit hitem3d.ai and click "Sign In" to create an account
2. **Upload an image**: click "Click to upload image" to upload your source image
3. **Choose a resolution**: select 1536P³ for the highest quality
4. **Enable textures**: turn on the Texture option for material effects
5. **Generate the model**: click the "Generate" button and wait a few minutes for generation to complete
6. **Download the model**: export in multiple formats, including OBJ, STL, and more

### Advanced Tips

**Image optimization tips:**
- Use high-resolution, clear source images
- Make sure the subject occupies a sufficient proportion of the image
- Avoid complex backgrounds; plain backgrounds work best
- Keep lighting even and avoid strong shadows that can interfere with detail recognition

**Ways to improve quality:**
- For complex objects, try generating from multiple angles and merging the results
- Use the texture feature to enhance visual realism
- Choose the appropriate model category for your use case

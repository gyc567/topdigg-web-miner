## Platform Overview

Grammarly is a globally leading AI-driven writing assistant platform that uses advanced artificial intelligence technology to help users improve their English writing quality. The platform provides real-time grammar checking, spelling correction, style suggestions, and intelligent writing enhancement services for individual users, students, professionals, and enterprise teams, and has become the world's most trusted smart writing tool.

**Core mission**: Democratize high-quality writing through AI technology, so that everyone can express their ideas with confidence

**Core value**: Leverage advanced natural language processing and machine learning technologies to deliver accurate, intelligent, and personalized writing improvement suggestions to users around the world.

## Product Overview and Core Features

### Smart Writing Assistant Feature Matrix

**Basic Writing Optimization**
- **Grammar check**: Real-time grammar error detection and correction suggestions
- **Spelling correction**: Intelligent spell checking, including context-aware word suggestions
- **Punctuation**: Punctuation usage standards and optimization suggestions

**Advanced Writing Enhancement**
- **Writing style optimization**: Adjusts tone and style based on writing purpose
- **Clarity improvement**: Sentence structure optimization to enhance expression clarity
- **Conciseness suggestions**: Redundancy detection and simplification suggestions

**AI-Driven Smart Features**
- **Tone detection**: Automatically detects and adjusts writing tone
- **Reader awareness**: Adjusts writing style according to the target reader

**Professional Writing Support**
- **Citation checking**: Academic writing citation format standards

- **Document insights**: Writing performance analysis and improvement suggestions
- **Brand tones**: Brand tone consistency maintenance for enterprises

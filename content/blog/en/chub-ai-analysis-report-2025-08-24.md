**Chub.ai** is a professional AI character chat platform focused on delivering highly customizable AI character creation and unrestricted conversation experiences. Built on advanced large language model technology, the platform supports multiple AI APIs including OpenAI and Anthropic Claude, enabling users to create unique AI characters and engage in free-form conversations. As a professional platform in the character AI space, Chub.ai has become the go-to choice for AI enthusiasts and developers thanks to its lack of censorship, powerful character customization features, and branching dialogue system.

## 🎯 Platform Overview and Core Value

### Why Did Chub.ai Become the Professional Choice in AI Character Customization in 2025?

In today's rapidly evolving world of AI conversations and character creation, **Chub.ai** provides users with a complete solution ranging from simple conversation to complex roleplay, built on its unique uncensored environment and professional-grade character customization tools:

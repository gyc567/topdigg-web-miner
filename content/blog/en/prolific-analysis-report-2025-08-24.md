## Platform Overview

Prolific is a leading platform focused on AI data collection and human participation, committed to providing high-quality human data services to researchers, AI developers, and organizations. Since its founding a decade ago, it has grown into one of the world's most trusted human data collection infrastructures.

**Core mission**: Helping thinkers, creators, and innovators turn the impossible into the possible

**Core value**: Delivering reliable, diverse human insight data for AI training, academic research, and product development through a network of 200,000+ verified global participants.

### Key Statistics
- Used by 35,000+ AI developers, researchers, and organizations
- A new study launches every 2 minutes
- 200,000+ active verified participants
- Coverage across 38+ countries
- Support for 80+ languages
- 97% study completion rate
- Datasets completed in under 2 hours on average

## Business Model Analysis

### Two-Sided Marketplace Model
Prolific employs a typical two-sided marketplace business model, connecting data demanders and data suppliers:

**Demand side (researchers/enterprises)**
- Academic research institutions
- AI technology companies
- Product development teams
- Market research agencies

**Supply side (participants)**
- 200,000+ verified users worldwide
- Diverse backgrounds and professional skills
- Paid for completing tasks (minimum £6/$8 per hour, recommended £9/$12 per hour)

### Fee Structure

**1. Platform model (pay as you go)**
- No monthly fee
- Enterprise clients: 42.8% platform fee
- Academic/nonprofit clients: 33.3% platform fee
- Participants paid hourly

**2. Managed services (customized)**
- Expert-led data collection
- Customized project pricing
- Includes complete datasets, expert curation, and multi-stage quality assurance

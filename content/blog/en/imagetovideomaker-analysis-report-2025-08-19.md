## 📚 Complete Usage Guide

### Quick Start for Beginners (Up and Running in 5 Minutes)

#### Method 1: Text-to-Video
1. Visit the [ImageToVideoMaker official website](https://imagetovideomaker.com)
2. Select the "Text to Video" feature
3. Enter a detailed video description (e.g., "a cute kitten chasing butterflies in a garden, cinematic shot, slow motion")
4. Choose the appropriate AI model (Veo3 for high quality, Kling for entertainment content)
5. Click "Generate" to start generation
6. Wait 3-5 minutes for processing to complete
7. Preview and download the generated video

#### Method 2: Image-to-Video
1. Click the "Image to Video" option
2. Upload a high-quality image (1080p or above recommended)
3. Choose the animation style and motion direction
4. Select a suitable AI model
5. Set video parameters and effects
6. Generate and download the video

### Advanced Usage Tips

#### Best Text Description Techniques
1. **Be Specific**: Use concrete actions, scenes, and emotions in your descriptions
2. **Camera Language**: Add professional terms like "close-up shot" and "bird's-eye view"
3. **Specify the Style**: Clearly state styles such as "cinematic" or "animated"
4. **Describe the Environment**: Detail lighting, weather, time, and other environmental factors
5. **Avoid Complex Scenes**: Keep single shots free of too many elements and complex interactions

#### Image Optimization Guide
1. **Resolution**: Use images at 1080p or higher resolution
2. **Composition**: Images with a clear subject and clean background work best
3. **Lighting**: Avoid images that are too dark or overexposed
4. **Format Selection**: PNG preserves the best quality; JPEG is also acceptable

#### Model Selection Strategy
1. **Veo3**: Choose when you need the highest quality; suited to professional production
2. **Kling**: For entertainment content and social media videos
3. **Hailuo**: For educational or tech content requiring physical accuracy
4. **Seedance**: For story-driven ads or narrative videos
5. **Wan**: For commercial brand videos that need a professional feel

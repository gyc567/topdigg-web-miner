**Fandom.com** is the world's largest fan-driven content platform, with more than 315 million monthly users and hosting 250,000 fan community wikis spanning games, movies, TV, anime, and other entertainment fields. As a quintessential example of user-generated content (UGC), Fandom has built the most comprehensive entertainment knowledge base in history through community collaboration.

## 🎯 Platform Overview and Core Value

### What Is Fandom?

**Fandom** (formerly known as Wikia) is a fan community platform built on MediaWiki technology that allows users to create, edit, and maintain detailed wiki pages for the entertainment content they love. The platform has become the digital hub of global fan culture, offering millions of users in-depth entertainment content information.

### Core Value Proposition

- **🌐 World's Largest Scale** - 250,000 active wiki communities, 40+ million content pages
- **🎮 Full Entertainment Coverage** - Comprehensive content across games, movies, TV, anime, and comics
- **👥 Community-Driven** - User-generated content created and maintained entirely by fans
- **🆓 Completely Free** - All content is fully free to access for users
- **🌍 Multi-Language Support** - Localized content in 80+ languages

## 🛠️ Platform Features and Technical Architecture

### Core Feature Modules

#### 1. Wiki Creation and Editing System
- **One-Click Wiki Creation**: Users can quickly create dedicated wikis for any entertainment content
- **Collaborative Editing Tools**: A powerful editor built on MediaWiki
- **Version Control**: Complete edit history and rollback functionality
- **Permission Management**: Multi-tiered user permissions and administrator system

#### 2. Content Discovery and Recommendation
- **FanCentral**: A personalized content discovery hub
- **Trend Tracking**: Real-time display of trending content and topics
- **Smart Recommendations**: A content recommendation algorithm based on user interests
- **Cross-Platform Integration**: Integrates authoritative media content from GameSpot, TV Guide, and others

#### 3. Community Interaction Features
- **User Profiles**: Personalized user homepages and achievement systems
- **Discussion Pages**: Every wiki page comes with a discussion section
- **Ratings and Voting**: Users can rate content quality
- **Social Sharing**: Seamless integration with major social media platforms

### Tech Stack Analysis

**Frontend technology**:
- **JavaScript framework**: Uses Webpack for module bundling
- **Responsive design**: Mobile-first adaptive layout
- **Performance optimization**: CDN acceleration, lazy loading of images, and other optimization techniques

**Backend infrastructure**:
- **MediaWiki core**: A PHP-based wiki engine
- **Database system**: MySQL cluster architecture
- **Search engine**: In-house search system with full-text search support
- **CDN network**: Globally distributed content delivery network

**Data analytics and advertising**:
- **Google Analytics**: In-depth user behavior analysis
- **Google Tag Manager**: Tag management and tracking
- **Programmatic advertising**: The in-house Fandomatic advertising platform

## 📚 Complete Usage Guide

### Quick Start for Beginners (Get Started in 3 Minutes)

#### Method One: Photo to Coloring Page
1. Visit the [GenColor.ai official website](https://gencolor.ai)
2. Click "Upload Photo" to upload your photo
3. Wait for the AI to process (5-10 seconds)
4. Preview the generated result
5. Choose PDF or PNG format to download

#### Method Two: Text Description Generation
1. Select "Text to Coloring Page" on the homepage
2. Enter your description (e.g., "a cute kitten playing in the garden")
3. Click "Generate" to start generation
4. Review the generated result and download

### Advanced Usage Tips

#### Best Photo Selection Guide
1. **Clarity Requirements**: Choose high-clarity photos with strong contrast
2. **Composition Advice**: Photos with prominent subjects and simple backgrounds work best
3. **Lighting Conditions**: Avoid photos that are too dark or too bright
4. **Complexity Control**: Simple compositions convert better than complex scenes

#### Text Description Optimization Tips
1. **Specific Descriptions**: Use specific, concise descriptive language
2. **Style Specification**: Add style terms such as "cartoon style" or "simple line drawing"
3. **Element Control**: Avoid describing too many elements; keep the theme focused
4. **Age Adaptation**: Adjust complexity for the target age group

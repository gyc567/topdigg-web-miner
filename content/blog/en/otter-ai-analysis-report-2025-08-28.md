## Platform Overview

Otter.ai is a leading AI-driven meeting assistant platform focused on delivering highly accurate real-time speech transcription and intelligent meeting summaries. Leveraging advanced artificial intelligence, the platform offers efficient meeting note-taking and action-item tracking solutions for professional users such as business teams, educators, and sales professionals.

**Core mission**: redefine meeting efficiency with AI technology so that every meeting produces actionable value

**Core value**: use advanced speech recognition and natural language processing to provide accurate, intelligent, and easy-to-use meeting assistant services to users worldwide.

### Key Statistics
- 95% transcription accuracy
- Real-time speech recognition and transcription
- Support for 20+ languages
- Integration with 20+ major collaboration platforms
- Intelligent meeting summary generation
- Automatic action item extraction
- Cross-platform sync support
- Voice search functionality

## Business Model Analysis

### Subscription-Based Business Model
Otter.ai uses a tiered subscription model that offers differentiated services to different user segments:

**Free Version (Basic)**
- 600 minutes of transcription per month
- Basic transcription features
- Maximum 30 minutes per recording
- 3 exports per month
- Basic search functionality

**Paid Subscription Plans**
1. **Business (US$20/month)**
   - 6,000 minutes of transcription per month
   - Maximum 4 hours per recording
   - Unlimited exports
   - Advanced search and keyword alerts
   - Custom glossary
   - Access to integrated platforms

2. **Enterprise (custom pricing)**
   - Unlimited transcription
   - Enterprise-grade security and compliance
   - Advanced administration and analytics
   - Dedicated customer support
   - SSO integration
   - Bulk user management

### Diversified Revenue Structure
- **Subscription revenue**: primary revenue source, roughly 80% of total revenue
- **Enterprise services**: customized solutions and integration services
- **API services**: transcription APIs for third-party developers
- **Training and consulting**: enterprise training and implementation consulting

## 🥇 🥈 Commodities

**Gold**: $4,183/oz (+1.34%)
Geopolitical risks stacked on top of expectations of global central bank easing, and gold set a stage high during the session. Tensions in the Middle East and the continuing Russia-Ukraine conflict pushed safe-haven money into gold ETFs, with $4,200 the near-term target.

**Silver**: $61.39/oz (+0.93%)
A recovery in industrial demand combined with safe-haven properties sent silver higher along with gold. The gold/silver ratio is still at historically low levels, so the catch-up potential in silver is worth watching.

## 🏛 Central Banks & Policy

🦅 **Federal Reserve**: The Fed raised rates by 25bp to 3.75%-4.00% at its September decision, approved 12-0, the first hike since 2023. The statement said inflation remains elevated and that the move aims to support a timely return to the 2% inflation target. On the economy, the labor market remains resilient, consumer spending is strong, and productivity growth is clear. **Comment**: The Fed's return to hiking exceeded market expectations, and both US equities and gold face repricing pressure in the short run.

🏰 **European Central Bank**: The ECB left rates unchanged in September, keeping the deposit facility rate at 2.50%. President Lagarde said eurozone economic growth is slowing but services inflation remains sticky. **Comment**: The policy divergence between the ECB and the Fed is widening, EUR/USD is under pressure, and the relative appeal of European assets has declined.

🇨🇳 **People's Bank of China**: The PBoC kept the LPR unchanged in September, with the 1-year rate at 3.00% and the 5-year-plus rate at 3.60%, marking the 16th consecutive month on hold. The central bank has recently injected net liquidity through open market operations, prioritizing FX stability over rate cuts. **Comment**: Domestic inflation is low but FX pressure exists, so the cautious stance continues. If the Fed sends clear easing signals in Q4, room for domestic rate policy may open up.

## 🌐 Diplomacy & Major Events

🗺 **China-US Diplomacy**: The two countries continue to compete in the trade and technology spheres. The US side has tightened export controls on AI chips and advanced process equipment to China, while China has restricted rare earth exports and stepped up coordination with US allies. High-level interaction between the two sides was limited during the September APEC meeting, and trade agreement talks progressed slowly. **Comment**: Tech decoupling is accelerating, supply chain restructuring in both countries continues, and competition in semiconductors and AI will shape the global industrial landscape for a long time.

🇪🇺 **China-EU Trade**: After the EU's tariffs on Chinese electric vehicles took effect, China launched anti-dumping investigations into EU goods such as brandy and pork. German automakers remain highly dependent on the Chinese market, and BMW, Mercedes and others continue to lobby the European Commission to reduce the size of the tariff increase. **Comment**: China-EU trade friction is spreading from electric vehicles into multiple sectors, but both sides have the will to keep the conflict under control, so a full trade war is unlikely.

⚔️ **Geopolitics**: Tensions in the Middle East continue to run high, the Iran nuclear deal talks have reached a deadlock, Houthi militants keep harassing Red Sea shipping, and navigation safety in the Strait of Hormuz is drawing global attention. Uncertainty about global energy supply is rising and the risk premium in crude oil has edged back up. **Comment**: The geopolitical risk premium will become an important support factor for commodities in Q4, so the evolution of the Middle East situation needs continued attention.

## 🏛 Central Banks & Policy

🦅 **Federal Reserve**: At its October 29 meeting, the FOMC cut rates 25bp for the second consecutive time, bringing the target range to 3.75%–4.00%. Chair Powell stressed "no urgency to act further," dampening market expectations for aggressive December easing.

🏰 **European Central Bank**: Lagarde has struck a cautious tone recently, emphasizing services-inflation stickiness. The market is pricing the ECB to stay on hold for the rest of 2026, with the October meeting expected to be a no-op.

🇨🇳 **People's Bank of China**: September LPR was held flat for the 16th consecutive month. Governor Pan Gongsheng said on Wednesday that policy will rely more on interest-rate tools and less on loan-growth targets. The market expects a Q4 25bp RRR cut plus additional structural tools.

## 🌐 Diplomacy & Major Events

🤝 **US–China Summit Diplomacy**: Trump and Xi held a three-day summit in Washington, agreeing to slash tariffs on $30 billion of goods in a phased trade framework that eased fears of further escalation. Asian stocks rallied on the day and the renminbi strengthened.

🇪🇺 **China–EU Trade**: EU members are still consulting on handling the expiration of anti-subsidy duties on Chinese EVs; some members favor prolongation. The window for resuming the Comprehensive Agreement on Investment talks has not opened.

🌍 **Geopolitics**: Tensions around the Strait of Hormuz have not eased materially, and Iran nuclear-talks remain stalled. Oil is holding in elevated ranges (above $80/bbl), indirectly supporting gold.

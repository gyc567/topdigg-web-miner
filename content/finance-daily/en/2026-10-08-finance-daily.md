## 🏛 Central Banks & Policy

🦅 **Fed**: The September FOMC is behind us; the market is now in "wait for data" mode, with the next FOMC as the main catalyst. Officials' recent comments have been broadly cautious, and views on the pace of cuts this year remain split.  
**Comment**: The dollar and UST yields lack a clear near-term direction; asset prices are driven more by data than by expectations.

🏰 **ECB**: The September meeting held rates steady; Lagarde emphasised "data dependence" and a "meeting by meeting" approach. Eurozone inflation has eased but services stickiness persists.  
**Comment**: EUR is range-bound below 1.12; the ECB is unlikely to deliver a directional steer anytime soon.

🇨🇳 **PBOC**: Pre-holiday open-market operations were broadly stable. The market is watching the post-holiday LPR fix and MLF rollover pace. The CNY fix has been hovering around 6.73, and the market still expects a relatively accommodative stance.  
**Comment**: Post-holiday liquidity and the LPR fix will be the next observation window; A-share and HK performance could in turn reshape policy expectations.

## 🌐 Diplomacy & Major Events

**China-US diplomacy**: Both sides have kept diplomatic and trade-engagement channels open, remaining in a "manage differences, seek consensus" mode on tariffs and tech. APEC and bilateral calls are the key upcoming windows.  
**Comment**: Near-term market sensitivity to US-China relations is fading, but any surprise statement could amplify volatility.

**China-EU trade**: The EU's anti-subsidy tariffs on Chinese EVs remain the focal point; price-undertaking negotiations and countermeasures continue to grind on. Member-state attitudes to the final package are the key near-term variable.  
**Comment**: Internal EU divisions leave the final package uncertain, a medium-term variable for Chinese NEV exporters.

**Geopolitics**: In the Middle East, Hormuz Strait transit risk remains the main tail risk for crude, with Iran nuclear-talk progress moving global energy prices. The Russia-Ukraine conflict continues to weigh on European energy and food supply chains.  
**Comment**: Geopolitical premia still support the medium-term bullish thesis for crude and gold, but event-driven swings stay elevated.
